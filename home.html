🛡️ Transparency & Google User Data Usage

Pigeon: To-Do Planner strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Purpose of Google Account Integration: Google Sign-In is used exclusively to authenticate you and connect with your personal Google Drive storage.
  • Google Drive AppData Scope: The app requests access only to its private Application Data folder (drive.appdata) in your Google Drive to upload, list, and restore your encrypted backup files. It cannot read, access, or modify any other files in your Drive.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Local Data Processing: All task computations, search indexes, and summaries run locally on your phone.
